Our Story

Founded in 2025, Built Around a Simple Idea

Rapid Water Heater LLC was founded in 2025 around a simple mission: provide honest, dependable, and high-quality service with the care and integrity only a local family business can offer. We understand how frustrating plumbing issues can be, especially when they affect your access to hot water — so we focus on clear communication, prompt response times, and solutions tailored to the needs of your home.

As a licensed Florida plumbing contractor, we hold ourselves to a high standard of professionalism on every project we take on. We work with trusted manufacturers such as Bradford White, Rheem, A.O. Smith, and Halo to provide products known for reliability, performance, and long-term value — because pairing quality equipment with professional installation is how homeowners get the most out of their investment.

We know that inviting a contractor into your home requires confidence and trust. Our team works to earn that trust through transparent pricing, honest recommendations, and dependable workmanship. That commitment is what our slogan is all about: Hot water now. No monkey business!
