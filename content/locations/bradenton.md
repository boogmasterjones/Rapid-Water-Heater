        <h3>Older Neighborhoods, Mid-Century Plumbing</h3>
        <p>Bradenton is one of the older cities on this stretch of coast, and its established neighborhoods have the plumbing to match. Homes built through the mid-twentieth century commonly have cast iron drain lines, and in the oldest of them, galvanized steel supply lines. Both have finite lifespans that many of these homes have now reached or passed.</p>
        <p>This shows up in predictable ways. Cast iron corrodes from the inside, gradually narrowing the pipe until drains that were fine for decades start backing up on a regular schedule — a pattern that no amount of clearing permanently fixes, because the pipe itself is the problem. Galvanized supply lines scale internally and restrict flow, which homeowners often mistake for a water heater problem when the pressure drops. Part of our job in these homes is telling you which of those you actually have, because replacing a water heater will not fix a supply line issue and we would rather you not spend money finding that out.</p>
        <h3>Surface Water and Coastal Conditions</h3>
        <p>Manatee County's public water supply comes substantially from surface water at Lake Manatee, supplemented by groundwater, while the City of Bradenton operates its own system drawing on Ward Lake. Either way, the water reaching most Bradenton homes carries enough dissolved mineral to leave sediment in the bottom of a tank water heater over the years, which is the usual culprit behind popping sounds and slower recovery in an older unit.</p>
        <p>Coastal exposure adds a second factor here that inland communities do not deal with to the same degree. Salt air is hard on exterior metal, and in homes near the water we pay closer attention to the condition of fittings, connectors, and the exterior of the tank itself. A unit that looks fine at a glance can have corrosion developing at the connections, and catching it during a routine visit is considerably cheaper than discovering it when a fitting fails.</p>

## How a Water Heater Installation Works in Bradenton

A replacement in a Bradenton home usually takes a few hours. We shut off the water and the gas or electric supply, drain the old tank, disconnect it and haul it away. The new unit gets fresh supply connections, a new temperature and pressure relief valve with its discharge line routed correctly, and a check of the drain pan. On a gas unit we make sure the venting draws properly instead of assuming the old setup still meets code. Then we fill the tank, purge the air from the lines and test the hot water at a tap before we leave.

In Manatee County a replacement usually needs a permit and an inspection, and we pull it. In the older homes near the river, the old installation was often not up to today's code, so the new one may need a pan, a corrected discharge line or an expansion tank. We tell you about those items in the quote, not after the work starts. We install Bradford White, Rheem, A.O. Smith and Halo units, and every new installation includes a free 1-year maintenance visit. More detail is on our [water heater installation](/services/water-heater-installation) page.

## Gas and Electric Water Heater Replacement in Bradenton

We replace gas and electric tanks all over Bradenton. If your home has gas, a new gas tank can usually go in the same spot, or we can talk through moving to a gas tankless unit. If your home is all-electric, the choice is a new electric tank or a heat pump (hybrid) unit, which collects heat from the surrounding air and moves it into the water. A hybrid needs room to breathe, so a garage suits it better than a small closed closet. In the bigger homes out toward State Road 64, we check whether the household has outgrown its old tank before we match it size for size.

## Water Heater Repair in Bradenton

Not every Bradenton call ends with a new tank. If the water has gone lukewarm, the pilot won't stay lit or the relief valve keeps dripping, the fix is often a single part: an element, a thermostat, a thermocouple or a valve. We test first and tell you whether a [water heater repair in Bradenton](/services/water-heater-repair) is worth it on a heater of that age, whether it runs on gas, electricity or is a tankless unit.
