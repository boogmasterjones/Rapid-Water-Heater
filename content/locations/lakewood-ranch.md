        <h3>A Master-Planned Community Reaching Replacement Age</h3>
        <p>Schroeder-Manatee Ranch began developing Lakewood Ranch in 1994, and the community has since grown across more than 35,000 acres spanning both Manatee and Sarasota counties. Because it was master-planned rather than assembled piecemeal, the housing stock is unusually consistent: homes built to modern code, on slabs, with water heaters typically in the garage and plumbing layouts that hold few surprises.</p>
        <p>The interesting consequence is timing. The earliest Lakewood Ranch villages are now thirty years old, which means many are on their second or even third water heater, while the newest neighborhoods are still on their original unit. A tank installed when a home was built in the mid-2000s is now squarely in the replacement window. We see a lot of that here — not failures so much as homeowners who checked the age, did the math, and decided to replace on their own schedule rather than wait for a cold shower.</p>
        <h3>Newer Homes, Different Opportunities</h3>
        <p>Lakewood Ranch's newer construction gives homeowners options that older housing stock often does not. A garage installation with good access makes a tankless conversion considerably more practical than it would be in a cramped interior closet, and the electrical and venting infrastructure in a recent build is more likely to support one without extensive modification. That does not make tankless automatically right — it depends on your household's simultaneous hot water use — but it does mean the conversation is worth having here.</p>
        <p>Much of Lakewood Ranch receives water through Braden River Utilities rather than directly from a county system, a legacy of how the community was developed. Whatever the source, Southwest Florida water carries enough dissolved mineral that sediment accumulates in a tank over the years, so we check for it during service and recommend an annual flush regardless of how new the home is. A newer house does not mean the water is any gentler on the equipment.</p>
