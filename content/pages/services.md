        <h2>The Difference Professional Water Heater Installation Makes</h2>
        <p>A water heater is one of the hardest-working systems in a Florida home, and how it's installed affects everything from energy bills to how long the unit lasts. Improper venting, undersized gas or electrical connections, and incorrect sizing are common causes of early failure and safety issues — problems that a licensed, experienced installer is trained to avoid. That's why every installation from Rapid Water Heater LLC follows manufacturer specifications and Florida plumbing code from the first shut-off valve to the final pressure test.</p>
        <p>We also take the time to size the system correctly for your household. A tank that's too small runs out of hot water during busy mornings; a tankless unit sized wrong for your fixture count won't deliver the flow rate your showers and appliances need. Getting this right the first time is the difference between a water heater that quietly does its job for a decade and one that becomes a recurring headache.</p>
