This Privacy Policy describes how Rapid Water Heater LLC ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses and protects your personal information when you use our website, call us, text us or request an estimate.

## Information we collect

When you contact us we may collect your name, phone number, email address, the address of the job and the details you give us about the work. Our website uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site; that data is aggregated and does not identify you.

## How we use it

We use your information to answer your request, give you an estimate, schedule and complete the work, and follow up afterwards. We only collect what you choose to give us when you call, text, email or fill in a form. Rapid Water Heater LLC is the sole owner of the information collected on this website. We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

## Text messages

If you give us your mobile number and check the consent box on our form, or reply YES to a text from us, we may send you customer care text messages: a callback confirmation, follow-ups on a request you made, appointment reminders, and one review request after a completed job. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to opt out and HELP for help. Consent to texts is not a condition of purchase.

**We will not share your mobile phone number or text message consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.** Our messages are sent through a service provider that handles delivery only.

## Sharing

We share information only with the service providers who help us run the business (for example scheduling, invoicing, website hosting and text message delivery), and only for that purpose. We may disclose information when the law requires it.

## Your choices

At any time, by calling us or using the form on our [contact page](/contact), you can:

- ask what information we have about you, if any;
- ask us to correct any details that are wrong;
- ask us to delete the details we hold about you;
- ask us to stop contacting you (for texts, you can also reply STOP);
- tell us about any concern you have with how we use your information.

## Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect your information. Our website is served over a secure, encrypted connection (look for "https" and the lock icon in your browser's address bar), so the details you send through our forms are encrypted on the way to us. Only the people who need your details to answer your request or do your job can see them, and we keep them in password-protected business systems. We do not ask for or collect credit card numbers through this website.

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new "last updated" date.

## Contact us

If you feel we are not following this Privacy Policy, please contact us right away: Rapid Water Heater LLC, (941) 876-5900, or the form on our [contact page](/contact).
