Finding Your Main Water Shut-Off

In most Southwest Florida homes, the main shut-off valve is located where the water line enters the house — commonly near the water heater, in a garage, or at an outdoor hose bib box near the street-facing side of the property. Turning this valve fully clockwise stops water flow to the entire home, which is the fastest way to limit damage from a burst pipe or major leak while you wait for help to arrive. If you're not sure where yours is located, it's worth finding it before an emergency happens — we're also happy to point it out during any routine visit.

What Counts as a True Plumbing Emergency

Not every plumbing issue needs an after-hours call. A slow drain or a dripping faucet can usually wait for a scheduled appointment. Active water leaks, sewage backups, a complete loss of water pressure throughout the home, or a water heater actively leaking near electrical components are the situations that warrant treating as an emergency — the kind where minutes matter for preventing structural or water damage. If you're not sure which category your situation falls into, call or text us and describe what's happening; we'll tell you honestly whether it can wait or needs immediate attention.
