Signs You Need a New Water Heater

Is It Time to Replace Your Water Heater?

It's 10+ Years Old

Most tank water heaters last 8-12 years. If yours is approaching or past that range, replacement before a failure is usually cheaper than an emergency swap.

Running Out of Hot Water

If hot water fades halfway through a shower, the unit may be undersized for your household or losing capacity as it ages.

Rust, Rumbling, or Leaks

Rusty water, popping/rumbling sounds from sediment buildup, or moisture around the base are warning signs the tank is failing.
